# Riddles for riddle puzzles. Format: english word = riddle
# The key is the English side of a word list line (the part before "=").
# The hero reads the riddle and types the word in the language they are
# learning, so one riddle works for every language. A word can have more
# than one riddle. Keep them short (under 110 characters), friendly, and
# never use the answer word itself.

## animals
dog = I wag my tail, fetch sticks and bark at the postman. What am I?
dog = I am called man's best friend, and I love a walk in the park.
cat = I purr when I am happy, I chase mice, and I always land on my feet.
cat = I have whiskers, I say "meow", and I nap in the sunniest spot.
horse = Knights ride me into battle. I have a mane, hooves, and I say "neigh".
horse = I pull carts and wear shoes made of iron. What am I?
bird = I have feathers and wings, I build a nest, and I sing in the morning.
fish = I live in water, I breathe with gills, and I swim with my fins.
dragon = I am a huge winged beast. I breathe fire and sleep on a pile of gold.

## home
house = I have walls, a roof and a front door. A family lives inside me.
book = I have a spine but no bones, and pages full of words to read.
door = You knock on me, open me and close me to go in or out of a room.
window = I am made of glass. You look through me to see outside.

## people
friend = I play with you, help you, and keep your secrets. Who am I?
sister = I am a girl with the same mother and father as you.
brother = I am a boy with the same mother and father as you.
mother = I am your mum. I looked after you from the day you were born.
father = I am your dad. I am married to your mum.
girl = I am a young female person. I am not yet a woman.
boy = I am a young male person. I am not yet a man.
child = I am young and still growing up. I go to school every day.
human being = I walk on two legs and I can talk. So can you, because you are one.
teacher = I stand at the front of the class and mark your homework.
farmer = I plough the fields, grow wheat and look after cows and sheep.
sailor = I work on a ship and sail across the sea.
king = I wear a crown and sit on a throne. I rule the whole land.
queen = I wear a crown and rule the land. I am married to the king.
master = I am in charge. The servants of the house must obey me.

## gods
god = People built temples for me and prayed to me. Zeus and Jupiter were two of us.
goddess = I am a female god, like Athena or Venus.
soul = I am the part of you that thinks and feels, and it is said I live forever.

## places
castle = I have towers, thick stone walls and a moat. Kings and knights live in me.
castle = I have a drawbridge and a keep. A lord and his knights live safely inside me.
forest = I am full of trees. Owls, deer and wolves live in me.
sea = I am wide, salty and full of waves. Ships sail across me.
road = People travel along me from town to town. The Romans built me straight.
field = I am open land where a farmer grows crops or keeps animals.
island = I am land with water all around me.

## things
sword = I am a long sharp blade that a knight carries into battle.
shield = A soldier holds me up to stop arrows and blows.
treasure = I am gold, jewels and riches hidden in a chest.
key = I am small and made of metal. Turn me in a lock to open it.
gold = I am a shiny yellow metal. Coins and crowns are made of me.
gift = I come wrapped in paper on your birthday. Someone gives me to you.
water = You drink me when you are thirsty. Rain and rivers are made of me.
sun = I rise in the east, shine all day and set in the west.
word = I am made of letters. You are learning lots of us right now.
war = I am a great fight between armies or countries. Peace is my opposite.

## verbs
I have = Say that something belongs to you: "____ a dog."
I write = Say what you do with a pen: "____ a letter."
I say = Say what you do when you speak words: "____ hello."
I love = Say how you feel about your best friend or your family: "____ you."
I see = Say what you do with your eyes: "____ a dragon!"
I am = Say who you are: "____ a hero."
